🏴‍☠️ What is Classroom Pirates?

Classroom Pirates is a team-based review game where students answer questions and choose squares on the game board to earn rewards, lose points, or trigger special events.

The goal is simple: earn as many points as possible for your team!

⚓ Getting Started

  1. Choose the number of teams. You can play with up to 6 teams.
  2. Choose how you want to play:
  • Start Game — starts a demo game so you can try the game immediately.
  • Load Lesson Pack — loads a lesson pack created for Classroom Pirates.
  • Create Lesson Pack — creates a new lesson pack using your own vocabulary, images, and content.

🎮 How to Play

  1. A Student from the current team will choose a X by make a sentence or two using the column and row headers.
  2. If the teacher is satisfied with the answer, the teacher will select the choosen X.
  3. The selected X reveals a random reward.
  4. After the reward is resolved, the turn moves to the next team.
Teacher tip: You can decide how students must answer questions before allowing them to choose a square. For example, students could answer individually, or work as a team.

🗺️ The Game Board

The board contains 36 squares arranged in a 6 × 6 grid.

Each square hides a random event.

Choose a square to discover what is hidden underneath.

💰 Rewards

Choosing a square can trigger one of several different rewards. Some rewards give points, while others can change the scores of the teams.

Coins

Coins give the current team 1–5 points.

The more coins you receive, the more points your team earns.

Broken Ships

A broken ship means the team loses points.

The team can lose between 1–3 points.

Treasure

Finding treasure gives the current team 6 points.

Treasure is rarer than ordinary coins, making it a valuable reward.

⚔️ Fight

A fight allows the current team to challenge another team.

The two teams choose who wins the fight. The winning team then receives a special effect.

Possible fight effects include:

  • Steal 3 points
  • Steal 5 points
  • Make the losing team lose all points
  • Give the winner 10 bonus points
  • Swap scores

💥 Reset

A reset sets every team's score to 0. This is a very rare event and can completely change the game.

⚔️ How Fights Work

  1. The current team receives a Fight reward.
  2. They choose another team as their opponent.
  3. The two teams compete in the fight.
  4. The winner receives the randomly selected fight effect.
Important: With the fight, the teacher only has to select the winner. The game engine only will apply the reward. With swap if the team with the highest score wins the scores will not be swapped. If the team with the lowest score wins then the scores will swap.

🧠 Strategy

Classroom Pirates isn't only about answering questions. Students also have to deal with the unpredictable rewards.

  • A team that is behind can suddenly catch up with a treasure or fight.
  • A team that is winning can lose points by discovering broken ships.
  • Fights can dramatically change the scoreboard.
  • A reset can completely change the game.

Because the rewards are hidden, every square is a gamble!

👩‍🏫 Tips for Teachers

  • Use the game after teaching a vocabulary or grammar point to review the lesson.
  • Require a correct answer before selecting an X for a team.
  • For speaking practice, require students to answer using a complete sentence.
  • Let teams discuss their answers before responding.
  • Use the unpredictable rewards to keep students interested even when one team is far ahead.

📚 Lesson Packs

Classroom Pirates can use custom lesson packs created specifically for the game.

A lesson pack can contain customized:

  • Instructions
  • Column headers
  • Row headers
  • Images

This allows teachers to adapt the game to different vocabulary, topics, and lessons.
