🧭 What is Pokémon Hunters?

Pokémon Hunters is a team-based review game. Teams answer questions to discover Pokémon, then choose a Poké Ball and try to catch one.

A successful catch adds XP to the team's score. Pick a ball with the catch chance and XP reward that best suits your team!

🎒 Getting Started

  1. Start Game uses the built-in sample questions so you can begin right away.
  2. Load Lesson Pack loads a saved Pokémon Hunters lesson for your class.
  3. Create Lesson Pack opens the lesson builder to make your own questions.
  4. Choose 2 to 6 teams, then start. The scoreboard tracks each team's XP.

🗺️ How to Play

  1. The current team picks an unused numbered or A/B/C pin on the map to open a question. If the team answers correctly, it reveals a Pokémon from that pin's assigned environment.
  2. Read the question to the team. Use Show Answer when they are ready, then mark the response correct or incorrect.
  3. An incorrect answer earns no XP and passes the turn to the next team.
  4. A correct answer reveals a Pokémon and three randomly selected Poké Balls. The Pokémon's name, HP, and defense appear in its stats card.
  5. Choose one Poké Ball to make a single catch attempt. The ball's glow and hover text show its odds and XP multiplier.
  6. A successful catch adds XP to the team's score. A missed catch awards 0 XP. Close the result screen to pass the turn.

✨ Poké Ball Guide: what the glow means

The colored glow around each ball tells you its type. A higher catch chance makes the Pokémon easier to catch; a higher XP multiplier gives more XP if the catch succeeds. Hover over a ball during play to see its exact stats.

⚪ Basic — White glow

70% catch chance · 1× XP
A steady, standard choice.

🟢 Good — Green glow

78% catch chance · 1.25× XP
A little more reward with a strong chance to catch.

🔵 Great — Blue glow

86% catch chance · 1.5× XP
The best catch chance among the standard balls, with a higher XP bonus.

🟣 Risky — Purple glow

55% catch chance · 2.25× XP
The hardest ball to land, but it gives the biggest XP multiplier.

🟡 Elite — Gold glow

100% catch chance · 1.75× XP
A guaranteed catch for a solid XP bonus.

📊 Pokémon Stats and XP

  • HP: This value determines the base XP reward.
  • Defense: Shown on the Pokémon card for reference. It does not currently affect the catch attempt.
  • XP calculation: Pokémon HP × ball's XP multiplier, rounded to the nearest whole point. XP is awarded only if the catch succeeds; a miss awards 0.

The name, HP, and defense card appears when the Pokémon is revealed. It stays visible for 20 seconds, then fades away.

💡 Quick Tips

  • Choose Great or Elite for a higher catch chance.
  • Choose Risky when your team wants to gamble for the biggest XP multiplier.
  • A ball can be used only once, so agree on your choice before clicking.
  • Press Escape on the question or result screen to end the turn without points.
