🔴🟡 What is Connect Four?

Connect Four is a two-team speaking game. Teams take turns making a sentence before choosing a column for one of their pucks.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Divide the class into Yellow and Red teams.
  2. The current team makes a sentence or gives a speaking response.
  3. After the teacher approves, click the puck above a column. It falls into the lowest open space.
  4. Speed: the first team to connect four wins immediately.
  5. Full Board: fill the board, then score each maximal run: 4 = 1 point, 5 = 2, 6 = 3, 7 = 4.
  6. Infinite: each connect four earns a point and starts another round until the teacher ends the game.
