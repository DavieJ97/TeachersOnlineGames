🔴🟡 What is 4 in a Row?

4 in a Row is a two-team speaking game. Teams take turns making a sentence or giving a speaking response before dropping one of their pucks into a column.

The goal is to connect four pucks in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

⚓ Getting Started

  1. Load a lesson pack, create one, or start a demo game.
  2. Divide the class into Yellow and Red teams.
  3. Choose a game mode, then select Start Game.

🎮 How to Play

  1. The current team makes a sentence or gives a speaking response.
  2. Once the teacher approves, click the puck above a column. It drops into the lowest open space.
  3. Teams take turns until the selected game mode ends.
Teacher tip: Decide how students should respond before each turn. They can answer individually or work together as a team.

🏆 Game Modes

  • Speed: the first team to connect four wins.
  • Full Board: fill the board, then score each maximal run of four or more: a run of 4 is worth 1 point, 5 is worth 2, 6 is worth 3, and 7 is worth 4.
  • Infinite: each connect four earns the team 1 point and starts another round. The game continues until the teacher ends it.
